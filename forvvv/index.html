About UNILAK Nyanza

Empowering International Students

UNILAK Nyanza Campus is home to a vibrant community of international students from across Africa and beyond. The International Student Representative (ISR) leadership team is here to support your academic journey and campus experience.

Through the ISIMS portal, students receive timely announcements, access academic resources, view campus events, and communicate directly with ISR leaders.
